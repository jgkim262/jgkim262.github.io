Jason Kim is a Ph.D. candidate in Accounting at the **[MIT Sloan School of Management](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/){: .extrabold}**, and he is on the <span class="jobmarket">2026–2027 academic job market</span>.

His [research](/research/){: .semibold} interests lie in <span class="medium">auditing</span>, <span class="medium">regulation</span>, and the <span class="medium">organizational design of accounting work</span>. He is particularly interested in how ownership structure and regulation shape audit quality and audit market competition.

Prior to his studies at MIT, he worked as an auditor at <span class="medium">KPMG Korea</span>. He holds an M.S. in Accounting and a B.A. in Business Administration from Seoul National University, and is a licensed <span class="medium">CPA</span> (inactive) in South Korea.
