Welcome! I am a Ph.D. candidate in accounting at the MIT Sloan School of Management, and I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.

My research interests lie in auditing, regulation, and the organizational design of accounting work. I am particularly interested in how ownership structure and regulation shape audit quality and audit market competition.

Previously, I worked as an auditor at KPMG Korea. I earned an M.S. in Accounting and a B.A. in Business Administration from Seoul National University, and am licensed as a CPA (inactive) in South Korea.

My [job market paper](/research/) examines whether allowing non-CPAs to hold an equity stake in CPA firms affects audit quality. You can reach me at [gwanhee@mit.edu](mailto:gwanhee@mit.edu).
